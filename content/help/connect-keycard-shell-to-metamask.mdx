# Connect Keycard Shell to MetaMask

### Step 1: Prepare the MetaMask app

1. Open the MetaMask app on your broswer or smartphone.
1. On top of the screen, click the current account name.
1. Select **Add account or wallet**.
1. Select **Hardware wallet**.
1. Select **QR-based**.
1. Select **Continue**.

### Step 2: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on. 
1. Enter your Keycard PIN.
1. Select **Connect software wallet** on Keycard Shell.
1. Select **Ethereum**.

### Step 3: Scan the QR code

1. On your computer or smartphone, allow MetaMask to access your camera if prompted.
1. Scan the QR code displayed on the Keycard Shell screen using your computer or smartphone camera.
1. In MetaMask, select the accounts you want to use in MetaMask and select **Unlock**.
