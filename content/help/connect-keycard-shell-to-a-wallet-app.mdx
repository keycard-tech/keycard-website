# Connect Keycard Shell to a wallet app

![](/assets/docs/connect-keycard-shell-to-a-wallet-app-header.png)

Keycard and Keycard Shell work together with a wallet app that supports QR signing to initiate and sign transactions. After [setting up your Keycard](./set-up-your-keycard), you need to connect your Keycard Shell to a [supported wallet app](/docs/supported-wallets). 

> ℹ️ **Note:** Currently, Keycard Shell doesn't appear on the official list of supported hardware wallets, but you can still connect it to a [supported wallet app](/docs/supported-wallets) by selecting the **QR-based** (for example, MetaMask) or **Keystone** (for example, Rabby and Nunchuk) option.

## What to expect

- The wallet app is an interface to manage your crypto assets. The private keys remain on your Keycard, and the wallet app has no access to them.
- You can connect your Keycard Shell to one or multiple wallet apps.
- You can [disconnect Keycard Shell from a wallet app](./disconnect-keycard-shell-from-a-wallet-app) at any time.
- You can use the wallet app on your phone, computer or browser, depending on what the app supports.
- Some wallet apps (for example, MetaMask) require you to create or import a recovery phrase before connecting Keycard Shell.

## About wallet app types

Before connecting to a wallet app, Keycard Shell asks you to select the type of wallet app you want to connect to. Here are the options:

- **Ethereum**: For wallet apps that support Ethereum and EVM-compatible networks, such as MetaMask.
- **Bitcoin**: For wallet apps that support Bitcoin, such as Bluewallet.
- **Bitcoin multisig**: Select when you want to use Bitcoin multisig with the wallet app.
- **Bitcoin testnet**: Select when you want to use Bitcoin testnet with the wallet app.
- **Multiple blockchains**: For wallet apps that support multiple blockchains, such as Bitget.

## Connect Keycard Shell to a wallet app

In this process, you use your wallet app to scan a QR code displayed on your Keycard Shell.

### Step 1: Prepare the wallet app

1. Install and open the wallet app on your device.
1. Optionally, create a new wallet account or import an existing one using a recovery phrase. This account doesn't affect the use of Keycard or Keycard Shell, and you don't have to use it.
1. Select the option for connecting a hardware wallet.
1. Select **QR-based** or **Keystone** depending on which one is available. 
1. Continue with the instructions in your wallet app to open the camera.

### Step 2: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on. 
1. Enter your Keycard PIN.
1. Select **Connect software wallet** on Keycard Shell.
1. Select the type of your wallet app.

### Step 3: Scan the QR code

1. Scan the QR code displayed on the Keycard Shell screen using the wallet app's camera.
1. In your wallet app, select the accounts you want to use in the wallet app.
1. Confirm to connect.

> ℹ️ **Note:** Steps may vary depending on your wallet app. Refer to your wallet's documentation for exact instructions on connecting a hardware wallet.
