# Receive crypto

Shell can help you quickly figure out which public addresses one Keycard protects. You just need to enter your Keycard in Shell. 

Insert your Keycard, enter your PIN and go to:
- Addresses > Ethereum
or
- Addresses > Bitcoin 

If you're using Ethereum, Shell will show you your BIP44 public addresses and the corresponding QR code. 

If you're using Bitcoin, Shell will show you your BIP84 native segwit bitcoin addresses.
