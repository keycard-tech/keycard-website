# Receive crypto

View your Keycard's public addresses on Shell.

1. Insert Keycard and enter your PIN.
2. Go to **Addresses** > **Ethereum** or **Addresses** > **Bitcoin**.

Shell displays the public adresses of your keycard, and indicates the index <n> on the bottom of the screen:
- Ethereum: BIP44 public address (and QR code) on path m/44'/60'/0'/0/<n>
- Bitcoin: BIP84 native SegWit address (and QR code) on path m/84'/0'/0'/0/<n>
