# Sign a transaction using Keycard Shell

![](/assets/docs/header-sign-a-transaction-using-keycard-shell.png)

Keycard and Keycard Shell work together with a wallet app that supports QR signing to initiate and sign transactions. You must [connect your Keycard Shell to a wallet app](./connect-keycard-shell-to-a-wallet-app.mdx) to initiate the transaction, then use your Keycard Shell to sign the transaction.

> ℹ️ **Note:** For a list of supported wallet apps, check out [Supported wallet apps](/content/docs/supported-wallets.mdx).

## What to expect

- With Keycard and Keycard Shell, you can sign transactions and interact with dApps through your software wallet. For example, you can send crypto, swap tokens, give dApps permission to use your tokens, or verify your identity.
- When signing a transaction, your Keycard or Keycard Shell doesn't use the internet. Keycard Shell transfers your transaction details and signature using QR codes.

## Sign a transaction using Keycard Shell

In this process, your wallet app shows a QR code with the transaction, which you scan with Keycard Shell. Then, you scan the Keycard Shell's QR code with your wallet to complete the transaction.

### Step 1: Initiate a transaction

If you have multiple accounts in your wallet app, be sure to select the one you want to use for the transaction, and confirm it comes from your Keycard.

1. Open your wallet app or dApp and initiate a transaction.
1. Confirm the transaction in your wallet app. You see a QR code containing your transaction data.

### Step 2: Sign the transaction with Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on. 
1. Enter your Keycard PIN.
1. Select **Scan QR transaction** to open the camera.
1. Scan the QR code in your wallet app with the Keycard Shell camera.
1. Review the transaction details on the Keycard Shell screen. Press ◀ **Left** and ▶ **Right** to view the full information.
1. Press **OK** to confirm the transaction. You see a QR code containing the signature on the Keycard Shell screen.

> ⚠️ **Caution:** Always verify the transaction details before confirming to ensure you're signing what you expect.

### Step 3: Send the signature to your wallet app

1. In your wallet app, click the option for receiving the transaction signature.
1.  Using your wallet app, scan the QR code on the Keycard Shell screen with your phone or webcam (depending on the device).

> ℹ️ **Note:** Steps may vary slightly depending on your wallet app. Refer to your wallet's documentation for exact instructions on initiating transactions with a hardware wallet.
