# Sign a transaction using Keycard Shell

![](/assets/docs/header-sign-a-transaction-using-keycard-shell.png)

The Keycard and Keycard Shell work together with a software wallet to complete transactions. You must [connect your Shell to a software wallet](./connect-keycard-shell-to-a-software-wallet.mdx) and use it to create the transaction, then use your Shell to sign the transaction.

> ℹ️ **Note:** To use your Shell with a dApp, connect them through a software wallet.

## What to expect

- With Keycard and Shell, you can sign transactions such as transferring crypto and interacting with dApps through your software wallet—for example, swapping tokens, approving contracts, or verifying your identity.
- When signing a transaction, your Keycard Shell or Keycard is not connected to the internet. Shell transfers your transaction details and signature using QR codes.

## Sign a transaction using Keycard Shell

### Step 1: Initiate a transaction

1. Open your software wallet or dApp and initiate a transaction using the account from your Keycard.
1. Confirm the transaction in your software wallet. You see a QR code containing your transaction data.

### Step 2: Sign the transaction with Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into the Shell to power it on. 
1. Enter your Keycard PIN.
1. Select **Scan QR transaction** to open the camera.
1. Scan the QR code in your software wallet with the Keycard Shell camera.
1. Review the transaction details on the Shell screen. Press ◀ **Left** and ▶ **Right** to browse pages.
1. Press **OK** to confirm the transaction. You see a QR code containing the signature on the Shell screen.

> ⚠️ **Caution:** Always verify the transaction details before confirming to ensure you're signing what you expect.

### Step 3: Send the signature to your software wallet

1. In your software wallet, click the option for receiving the transaction signature.
1. Scan the QR code on the Keycard Shell screen with your software wallet camera.

> ℹ️ **Note:** Steps may vary slightly depending on your software wallet. Refer to your wallet's documentation for exact instructions on initiating transactions with a hardware wallet.
