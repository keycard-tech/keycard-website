# Set an auto turn-off time

Shell automatically turns off after inactivity. Turn it back on with the side button or by removing and reinserting your Keycard.

Default: 3 minutes

Change it: **Settings** > **Shell** > **Auto-off time**

Options: 3, 5, 10, 30 minutes, or Never
