# Charge your Shell

Charge your Shell with any USB-C charger.

If you don't trust your charger or computer's USB-C port, [disable USB data](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) to keep your Shell air-gapped while charging.

Your Shell draws a maximum of 500mA and doesn't engage in USB-C power negotiations.
