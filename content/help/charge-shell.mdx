# Charge Shell

You can charge Shell with any USB-C charger. If you don't trust your charger or your computer USB-C port, you can [disable USB data](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) to keep your Shell air-gapped while charging. 

Shell does not engage in USB-C negotiations with its power supply and will draw a maximum of 500mA from the power supply.
