# Replace your Keycard Shell battery

Keycard Shell uses a replaceable 900mAh BL-4C battery, designed to last for years. The battery is also resistant to water and dust. You can find the battery in many electronics stores or online.

## Remove the battery

![](/assets/docs/replace-your-keycard-shell-battery-groove.png)

1. Remove the Keycard from the Keycard Shell to turn it off.
2. Gently press and slide the back cover downward to remove it.
3. Lift the battery out using the groove in the battery slot. You can use a small tool or the corner of your Keycard to help pry it out.

## Insert the new battery

![](/assets/docs/replace-your-keycard-shell-battery-battery.png)

1. Insert the new battery into the slot, ensuring the contacts align correctly.
2. Slide the back cover until it clicks into place.   

> 💡 **Tip:** If your Shell doesn't power on when you insert the Keycard, check the battery alignment or connect a USB-C cable to charge it.
