# Set up your Keycard

![Header image showing a Keycard inserted into a Keycard Shell.](/assets/docs/set-up-your-keycard-header.png)

To start using a new Keycard, you need to set a Keycard PIN and optionally a duress PIN, then add a key pair to your Keycard with the Keycard Shell.

The Keycard PIN functions as a password for your Keycard. A duress PIN can unlock a decoy wallet. Check out [About your Keycard PIN and duress PIN](./about-your-keycard-pin-and-duress-pin) to learn more.

## Set up your Keycard

Before you start, [get to know your Keycard Shell](./get-to-know-your-keycard-shell)'s controls.

### Step 1: Set your Keycard PIN and duress PIN

If you skip setting up a duress PIN, you cannot add one later. This restriction prevents attackers from forcing you to reveal it.

Be sure to use different numbers for your Keycard PIN and duress PIN.

1. Insert the Keycard with the chip facing up and push it in all the way. Your Keycard Shell powers on automatically.
1. Set a Keycard PIN and press **OK**.
1. Enter the Keycard PIN again and press **OK** to confirm.
1. Optionally, press and hold **OK** to set a duress PIN. Press **C** to skip this step.
1. Set a duress PIN and press **OK**.
1. Enter the duress PIN again and press **OK** to confirm.
1. Press **OK**.

### Step 2: Add a key pair

There are three methods to add a key pair to your Keycard: generate a new key pair, import with a recovery phrase, or import with a QR code.

A passphrase is an optional extra word or phrase you add to your recovery phrase for extra security. If you set one, you must remember it to recover your wallet.

SLIP39 is an advanced backup method that splits your wallet backup into multiple parts. However, it is not yet widely supported. For more information, check out [Understand the SLIP39 wallet backup standard](./understand-the-slip-39-wallet-backup-standard).

#### Generate a new key pair

1. On your Keycard Shell, select **Generate new key pair**.
1. Choose your recovery phrase type: 12 or 24 words, or a SLIP39 backup. You can also select whether to use a passphrase. Then press **OK** to confirm the text on the screen.
1. Skip this step if you choose a 12- or 24-word recovery phrase. If you choose a SLIP39 backup, press ◀ ▶ **Left** / **Right** to set the total number of parts and the threshold number of parts required to recover your wallet. Then press **OK**.
1. Write down the recovery phrase(s) and press **OK**.
1. Press **OK** to confirm the text on the screen. Then confirm your recovery phrase by selecting the words as required.
1. If you choose to set a passphrase, press ▲ ▼ **Up** / **Down** and ◀ ▶ **Left** / **Right** to select letters and set a passphrase. Press and hold **OK** to confirm.
1. Press **OK**.

> ⚠️ **Caution** 
>
> Keep your recovery phrase(s) safe and private. The Keycard team will never ask you for this information, and anyone who does is trying to steal your funds.

#### Import with recovery phrase 

1. On your Keycard Shell, select **Import recovery phrase**.
1. Select the type of your recovery phrase (12 or 24 words, or a SLIP39 backup) and whether it includes a passphrase.
1. Press ▲ ▼ **Up** / **Down** and ◀ ▶ **Left** / **Right** to select letters and enter each word of your recovery phrase.
1. If there is a passphrase, enter it. Press and hold **OK** to confirm.
1. Press **OK**.

#### Import with QR code

1. On your wallet app or another hardware wallet, prepare your recovery phrase QR code for scanning.
1. On your Keycard Shell, select **Scan QR**.
1. If your recovery phrase has a passphrase, select **Add passphrase**; otherwise, select **No passphrase**.
1. Scan the QR code with your Keycard Shell's camera.
1. Confirm your recovery phrase by pressing **OK**.
1. If there is a passphrase, press ▲ ▼ **Up** / **Down** and ◀ ▶ **Left** / **Right** to select letters and enter the passphrase. Press and hold **OK** to confirm.
1. Press **OK**.

> 💡 **Tip** 
>
> After setup, your Keycard is ready. To start managing your crypto, [connect your Keycard Shell to a wallet app](./connect-keycard-shell-to-a-wallet-app.mdx) that supports QR signing to initiate and sign transactions.
