# About Keycard and Keycard Shell

![Header image showing Keycard and Keycard Shell devices](/assets/docs/about-keycard-and-keycard-shell-header.png)

The Keycard and Keycard Shell provide hardware-level security for your crypto wallet's private keys. These private keys are the fundamental cryptographic component necessary for accessing and controlling your crypto funds.

## What is Keycard?

Keycard is a secure hardware wallet in the form of a contactless smart card. It stores your private keys on a dedicated secure chip, keeping your crypto safe even when used with internet-connected devices.

Key features:
- **Secure chip**: Your private keys never leave the card
- **NFC support**: Communicate wirelessly with mobile devices
- **Open source**: Adapt Keycard to your own [projects ↗](https://github.com/keycard-tech/keycard-ecosystem-projects/)
- **Status integration**: Supported in [Status ↗](https://status.app/help/keycard/keycard-your-quick-start-guide#manage-your-keycard) mobile and desktop apps

Keycard works with iOS and Android mobile phones through NFC and with desktop computers using a USB smartcard reader.

## What is Keycard Shell?

Keycard Shell is a companion device that adds extra functionality to Keycard, enabling interaction with more wallet apps and signing transactions via QR codes.

Keycard Shell must work together with Keycard. You can share your Keycard Shell with friends and family to use with their own Keycards, because it doesn't store any private keys or sensitive information.

Key features:
- **Display**: See transaction details clearly on the screen
- **Wider wallet support**: Works with MetaMask, BlueWallet, Rabby, imToken, [and more](https://keycard.tech/wallets)
- **Air-gapped operation**: [Sign transactions](./sign-a-transaction-using-keycard-shell) via QR codes, keeping your private keys offline
- **SLIP39 support**: [Generate and restore wallets](./set-up-your-keycard-with-keycard-shell#step-2-add-a-master-key-pair) using SLIP39 recovery phrases
- **Keycard management**: [Change PIN](./change-your-keycard-pin-or-duress-pin), [unblock](./unblock-your-keycard-using-keycard-shell), and [factory reset](./factory-reset-your-keycard-using-keycard-shell) your Keycard
- **Open source**: Firmware and hardware are open source. Advanced users can [build a DIY Keycard Shell](/developers/diy-keycard-shell).

<Admonition type="note">
    Keycard and Keycard Shell are deliberately air-gapped and have no connection to the blockchain. The wallet app you use with Keycard or Keycard Shell constructs the complete transaction and only asks the hardware to sign it, keeping your private keys offline.
</Admonition>
