# About your Keycard PIN and duress PIN 

![Header image of the Keycard Shell's screen displaying the PIN setup process.](/assets/docs/about-your-keycard-pin-and-duress-pin-header.png)

When [setting up your Keycard](./set-up-your-keycard), you must create a six-digit Keycard PIN (personal identification number). It functions like a password to protect access to your Keycard.

Optionally, you can set a duress PIN to unlock a decoy wallet if someone forces you to unlock your Keycard.

<Admonition type="note">
    Keycard Shell prevents you from setting your Keycard PIN and duress PIN to be the same.
</Admonition>

## About your Keycard PIN

Every time you insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell, you must enter your Keycard PIN to unlock it.

The PIN protects access to the Keycard itself, not the funds directly. Your recovery phrase protects access to your funds.

You can [change your Keycard PIN](./change-your-keycard-pin-or-duress-pin) at any time.

## About your duress PIN

After setting your Keycard PIN, you can choose to set a duress PIN.

<Admonition type="note">
    If you skip setting a duress PIN, you can't add one later. This limitation is unavoidable to make the duress PIN undetectable to attackers. To use a duress PIN, you must [factory reset your Keycard](./factory-reset-your-keycard.mdx) and [set up your Keycard](./set-up-your-keycard.mdx) again.
</Admonition>

When you set a duress PIN, your Keycard automatically creates a decoy wallet. There is no visible way to tell whether you are using the decoy wallet or the regular wallet, or whether a decoy wallet exists. Only you know if you set a duress PIN.

The duress PIN works the same way as your regular Keycard PIN, and the decoy wallet behaves like a regular wallet. You can also send and receive funds using the decoy wallet. It's intended to be a wallet with low value to show to an attacker. You must manually transfer funds into the decoy wallet if you want it to appear to hold value.

Your decoy wallet and regular wallet are linked to the same recovery phrase. If you recover your wallet on another Keycard, you restore both wallets at the same time.

[Changing the duress PIN](./change-your-keycard-pin-or-duress-pin) doesn't affect the regular Keycard PIN.

## Choose a strong PIN

Following these tips can help you create a secure PIN for your Keycard:

- Avoid simple sequences (for example, 123456) or repeated digits (for example, 111111). Instead, use a mix of numbers that are not easily guessable.
- Do not use easily obtainable information, such as your birthdate, phone number, or address.
- Use a unique PIN for your Keycard that you do not use for other accounts or devices.
- Consider changing your PIN periodically to enhance security.

## If you forget your PIN

After three wrong attempts at entering your Keycard PIN or duress PIN, your Keycard becomes blocked. You can unlock it by entering your [PUK](./set-a-keycard-puk) (personal unblocking code).

If you don't have a PUK, you can [factory reset](./factory-reset-your-keycard) your Keycard and then [import your wallet accounts](./set-up-your-keycard.mdx#add-a-key-pair) again. This doesn't affect the funds in your wallet accounts. You can always access your funds with your recovery phrase.
