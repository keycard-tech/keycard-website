# Get to know your Keycard Shell

## What's in the box

- Keycard Shell
- Battery
- 2 Keycards

## Hardware overview

![](/assets/docs/get-to-know-your-keycard-shell-front.png)

![](/assets/docs/get-to-know-your-keycard-shell-back.png)

1. TFT color display - Shows menus and transaction details
2. Power button - Turns the device on or off
3. Numeric keypad - For input and navigation
4. Camera - Scans QR codes for signing and verification
5. Smart card reader - Reads Keycards
6. USB-C port - Used for charging and firmware updates
7. Battery slot - Holds one Nokia BL-4C battery
8. Back cover - Protects the battery slot and holds the Keycard in place on the reader

## Move and select with the keypad

<br />
![](/assets/docs/get-to-know-your-keycard-shell-keypad.png)

1. Press ▲ ▼ **Up / Down** and ◀ ▶ **Left / Right** to move through menu options on the Keycard Shell screen
2. Press **C** to cancel an action or go back to the previous screen
3. Press **OK** to confirm an action or select an option

## Battery

Keycard Shell uses a replaceable 900mAh BL-4C battery, designed to last for years. The battery is also resistant to water and dust. You can find the battery in many electronics stores or online.

If the battery fails during the Shell's warranty period, please contact us at [support@keycard.tech](mailto:support@keycard.tech) for support.

You can charge the Shell using any standard USB-C cable.
   
For step-by-step instructions on replacing the battery, see [Replace your Keycard Shell battery](./replace-your-keycard-shell-battery.mdx).
