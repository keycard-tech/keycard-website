# Update Keycard Shell online

Regularly updating your Keycard Shell firmware and database keeps your device current with the latest features and security improvements.

<Admonition type="note">
    To update your Keycard Shell in air-gapped mode, read [Update Keycard Shell in air gapped mode](update-keycard-shell-in-air-gapped-mode).
</Admonition>

## Update Keycard Shell online

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer with a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge or Opera) to access the [update page ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update). Other browsers cannot connect to USB devices. To update without one of these browsers, read [Update Keycard Shell in air gapped mode](./update-keycard-shell-in-air-gapped-mode).

Make sure you [enable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell before starting the update process.

<Admonition type="note">
   If you use Linux, you must add the following udev rule to the file `/etc/udev/rules.d/50-keycardshell.rules` to grant the required permissions.

   ```Markdown
   ACTION!="remove", KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="21f7", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
   ```
</Admonition>

### Step 1: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on.
2. Enter your Keycard PIN.
3. Connect your Keycard Shell to your computer using a USB-C cable.
4. Allow the connection request if you see a security alert from your computer.

### Step 2: Prepare the update page

The update page displays the latest available firmware and database versions. Your Keycard Shell may already be up to date on one or both of these versions.

1. On your computer, open the update page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/update](https://shell.keycard.tech/update). Before you continue, confirm the URL is exactly `https://shell.keycard.tech/update`.
1. Click **Update**.
1. In the **shell.keycard.tech wants to connect to a HID device** prompt window, select your Keycard Shell device and click **Connect**.
1. The latest firmware and database version are automatically selected. Unselect the updates you don't want to install.

### Step 3: Update your Keycard Shell

The update page installs the firmware first, then the database. Your Keycard Shell asks you to approve each one twice: once to receive it, and once to install it.

1. On the update web page, click **Transfer to Device**.
2. On your Keycard Shell, press **OK** to receive the firmware.
3. When the transfer ends, press **OK** again to install it. Your Keycard Shell restarts.
4. Enter your Keycard PIN on your Keycard Shell.
5. If you also selected the database, click **Transfer database** on the update web page. If your browser asks again, select your Keycard Shell and click **Connect**.
6. On your Keycard Shell, press **OK** to receive the database.
7. When the transfer ends, press **OK** again to install it. Your Keycard Shell does not restart after the database update.
