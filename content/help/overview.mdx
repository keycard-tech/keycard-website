# Overview

Welcome to Keycard Help! This guide will help you set up, use, and troubleshoot your Keycard and Keycard Shell hardware wallets.

## What is Keycard?

Keycard is a secure hardware wallet in the form of a contactless smart card. It stores your private keys on a dedicated secure chip, keeping your cryptocurrency safe even when used with internet-connected devices.

**Key features:**
- **Secure chip**: Your private keys never leave the card
- **Contactless**: Works with NFC-enabled devices
- **Open source**: Transparent and community-audited technology
- **Status integration**: Natively supported in Status apps

Keycard works with iOS/Android mobile phones through NFC and with desktop computers using a USB smartcard reader.

## What is Keycard Shell?

Keycard Shell is a companion device that makes your Keycard even more powerful and user-friendly. It's a hardware wallet case that you insert your Keycard into, adding:

**Enhanced features:**
- **Display**: See transaction details clearly
- **Keypad**: Enter PINs securely
- **Camera**: Scan QR codes for transactions
- **USB connectivity**: Connect to computers and mobile devices
- **Wider wallet support**: Works with MetaMask, BlueWallet, Rabby, imToken, and more

Keycard Shell bridges the gap between Keycard's security and wallets that don't support NFC, making hardware wallet security accessible to more users.

## Security First

Your Keycard and Keycard Shell are designed with security as the top priority:
- Private keys are generated and stored securely on the card's chip
- All communication is encrypted
- You maintain full control of your funds
- No personal data is collected or stored by the devices
