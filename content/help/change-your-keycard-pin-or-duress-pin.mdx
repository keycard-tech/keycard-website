# Change your Keycard PIN or duress PIN

When [setting up your Keycard](./set-up-your-keycard), you must set a six-digit Keycard PIN (personal identification number). It functions like a password to protect access to your Keycard.

Optionally, you can set a duress PIN to unlock a decoy wallet if someone forces you to unlock your Keycard. Check out [About your Keycard PIN and duress PIN](./about-your-keycard-pin-and-duress-pin) to learn more.

> ℹ️ **Note:** 
>
> If you skip setting the duress PIN, you can't add one later. To use a duress PIN, you must [factory reset your Keycard](./factory-reset-your-keycard.mdx) and [set up your Keycard](./set-up-your-keycard.mdx) again.

## Change your Keycard PIN or duress PIN

Changing your Keycard PIN or duress PIN follows the same procedure.

1. Insert the Keycard into Keycard Shell with the chip facing up and push it in all the way. Your Keycard Shell powers on automatically.
1. Enter your Keycard PIN or duress PIN depending on which one you want to change.
1. On Keycard Shell, select **Settings** > **Keycard** > **Change PIN**.
1. Enter the new PIN and press **OK**.
1. Enter the new PIN again and press **OK** to confirm.
1. Press **OK**.

> ℹ️ **Note:** 
>
> Keycard Shell prevents you from setting your Keycard PIN and duress PIN to be the same.
