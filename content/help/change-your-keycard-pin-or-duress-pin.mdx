# Change your Keycard PIN or duress PIN

The Keycard PIN functions as a password for your Keycard. A duress PIN can unlock a decoy wallet. Check out [About your Keycard PIN and duress PIN](./about-your-keycard-pin-and-duress-pin) to learn more.

> ℹ️ **Note:** 
>
> Keycard Shell prevents you from setting your Keycard PIN and duress PIN to be the same. Otherwise, entering a shared PIN would only grant access to your decoy wallet.

## Change your Keycard PIN or duress PIN

Changing your Keycard PIN or duress PIN follows the same procedure.

1. Insert the Keycard with the chip facing up and push it in all the way. Your Keycard Shell powers on automatically.
1. Enter your Keycard PIN or duress PIN depending on which one you want to change.
1. On Keycard Shell, select **Settings** > **Keycard** > **Change PIN**.
1. Enter the new PIN and press **OK**.
1. Enter the new PIN again and press **OK**.
1. Press **OK**.
