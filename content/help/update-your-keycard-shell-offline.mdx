# Update your Keycard Shell offline

Updating your Keycard Shell offline helps maintain the security of your device by minimizing its exposure to online threats during the update.

<Admonition type="note">
    To update your Keycard Shell via the browser, read [Update your Keycard Shell online](update-your-keycard-shell-online).
</Admonition>

## Update your Keycard Shell offline

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer to access the [update files ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped), a USB drive to transfer the files, and an air-gapped computer (a computer that is not connected to the internet) to complete the update.

Make sure you [enable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell before starting the update process.

<Admonition type="note">
   If you use Linux, you must add the following udev rule to the file `/etc/udev/rules.d/50-keycardshell.rules` to grant the required permissions.

   ```Markdown
   ACTION!="remove", KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="21f7", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
   ```
</Admonition>

## Step 1: Download the updater and update files

1. Open the update page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/update ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update).
2. Click **Update in air-gapped mode**.
3. Click **Download Shell Updater**. To download for a different operating system, click **Expand ⌵** and select the preferred option.
4. Click **Download firmware**.
5. Click **Download database**. 
6. Optionally, verify the integrity of the downloaded files using the SHA256 checksums shown on the update page.
7. Transfer the downloaded files to your air-gapped computer using a verified and secure USB drive.

## Step 2: Update your Keycard Shell

1. On your air-gapped computer, unzip and run the updater application.
2. Under **Database Update**, click **Select local file** and select the database file on your USB drive.
3. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the database update by pressing **OK**.
4. Return to the updater,  click **Back to home**. 
5. Under **Firmware Update**, click **Select local file** and select the firmware file on your USB drive.
6. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the firmware update by pressing **OK**.

<Admonition type="tip">
    After the update, [disable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell to ensure it remains air-gapped.
</Admonition>
