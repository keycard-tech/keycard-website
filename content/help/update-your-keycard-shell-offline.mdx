# Update your Keycard Shell offline


<Admonition type="note">
    To update your Keycard Shell via the browser, read [Update your Keycard Shell online](update-your-keycard-shell-online.mdx).
</Admonition>

## What to expect

- trun on data transfer

## Step 1: Download the updater and update files


In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer to access the [update files ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update), a USB drive to transfer the files, and an air-gapped computer (a computer that is not connected to the internet) to complete the update process.

1. Open the update page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/update/ ](https://shell.keycard.tech/update/).
1. Click **Update in air-gapped mode**.
1. Click **Download Shell Updater**. Click **Expand ** if you want to download for a different OS.
1. Click **Download firmware**. 
1. Click **Download database**. 
1. Copy the downloaded file to a USB drive that doesn't contain any other files.
1. Transfer the updater file to an air-gapped computer using the USB drive.

1. Unzip the downloaded file, and run the updater application.
1. In **Database Update**, click **Select local file** and choose the database file you downloaded earlier.
1. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the database update by pressing **OK**.
1. Return to the updater,  click **Back to home**. 
1. In **Firmware Update**, click **Select local file** and choose the firmware file you downloaded earlier
1. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the firmware update by pressing **OK**.
