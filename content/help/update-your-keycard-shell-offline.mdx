# Update your Keycard Shell offline

Updating your Keycard Shell offline helps maintain the security of your device by minimizing its exposure to online threats during the update.

<Admonition type="note">
    To update your Keycard Shell via the browser, read [Update your Keycard Shell online](update-your-keycard-shell-online.mdx).
</Admonition>

## Update your Keycard Shell offline

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer to access the [update files ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update), a USB drive to transfer the files, and an air-gapped computer (a computer that is not connected to the internet).

Make sure you [enable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell.mdx) on your Keycard Shell before starting the update process.

<Admonition type="note">
   If you use Linux, you must add the following udev rule to the file `/etc/udev/rules.d/50-keycardshell.rules` to grant the required permissions.

   ```Markdown
   ACTION!="remove", KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="21f7", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
   ```
</Admonition>

## Step 1: Download the updater and update files

The update page displays the latest available firmware and database versions. Your Keycard Shell may already be up to date on one or both of these versions. Download the update you need to install.

1. Open the update page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/update ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update). Before you continue, confirm the URL is exactly `https://shell.keycard.tech/update`.
1. Click **Update in air-gapped mode**.
1. Click **Download Shell Updater**. To download for a different operating system, click **Expand ⌵** and select the preferred option.
1. Click **Download firmware**.
1. Click **Download database**. 
1. Optionally, verify the integrity of the downloaded files using the SHA256 checksums shown on the update page.
1. Transfer the downloaded files to your air-gapped computer using a verified and secure USB drive.

## Step 2: Update your Keycard Shell

1. On your air-gapped computer, unzip and run the updater application.
1. Under **Database Update**, click **Select local file** and select the database file on your USB drive.
1. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the database update by pressing **OK**.
1. Return to the updater,  click **Back to home**. 
1. Under **Firmware Update**, click **Select local file** and select the firmware file on your USB drive.
1. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the firmware update by pressing **OK**.

<Admonition type="tip">
    After the update, [disable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell.mdx) on your Keycard Shell to ensure it remains air-gapped.
</Admonition>
