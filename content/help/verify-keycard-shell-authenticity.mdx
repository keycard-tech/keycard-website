# Verify Keycard Shell authenticity

Before you use your Keycard Shell, verify that it is genuine. A genuine Shell was made by us, so it runs only firmware that we signed.

<Admonition type="note">
    Keycard Shell verification is optional, but we strongly advise verifying your device before using it. This is a security measure designed to protect you from using a fake or compromised device.
</Admonition>

## Verify Keycard Shell authenticity

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer or mobile device to access the [verification page ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/verify) to complete the verification process.

### Step 1: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on. 
2. Enter your Keycard PIN.
3. Select **Settings** > **Shell** > **Verify**.
4. Press **OK** to confirm the text on the screen.
5. Press **OK** again to open the camera on your Keycard Shell.

### Step 2: Verify your Keycard Shell

1. On your computer or mobile device, open the verification page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/verify ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/verify). Before you continue, confirm the URL is exactly `https://shell.keycard.tech/verify`.
2. When the QR code on the web page is sharp, scan it with the camera on your Keycard Shell. Your Keycard Shell then shows a QR code.
3. Select **Continue** on the web page.
4. If your browser asks for camera access, select **Enable camera**.
5. Scan the QR code on your Keycard Shell with the camera on your computer or mobile device. The web page shows the result.

<Admonition type="note">
   The web page sends your Keycard Shell a one-time challenge. Your Keycard Shell signs it with a key unique to each device, recorded when we made it. The web page checks the signature in your browser against our signed list of produced Shells. The list holds no device IDs or keys, only hashes of them. No Keycard server takes part. A genuine Shell has our bootloader, and our bootloader runs only firmware we signed.
</Admonition>

<Admonition type="note">
   On firmware v1.5.2 or older, your Keycard Shell waits for one more QR code after step 5. There is none: press **Back** on your Keycard Shell. Then [update your Keycard Shell](./update-keycard-shell-online).
</Admonition>

<Admonition type="caution">
    If the web page says your Keycard Shell is not on our produced units list, stop using it and contact the Keycard team [on Discord ↗](https://discord.gg/uJAXk7jFhZ).
</Admonition>
