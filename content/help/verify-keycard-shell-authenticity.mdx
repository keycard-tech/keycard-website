# Verify Keycard Shell authenticity

Before using your Keycard Shell, verify its authenticity to ensure it has not been tampered with and it runs authentic firmware.

<Admonition type="note">
    Keycard Shell verification is optional, but we strongly advise verifying your device before using it. This is a security measure designed to protect you from using a fake or compromised device.
</Admonition>

## Verify Keycard Shell authenticity

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer or mobile device to access the [verification page ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/verify) to complete the verification process.

### Step 1: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on. 
2. Enter your Keycard PIN.
3. Select **Settings** > **Shell** > **Verify**.
4. Press **OK** to confirm the text on the screen.
5. Press **OK** again to open the camera on your Keycard Shell.

### Step 2: Verify your Keycard Shell

1. On your computer or mobile device, open the verification page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/verify ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/verify).
2. Scan the QR code on the web page with the camera on your Keycard Shell.
3. Select **Continue** on the web page.
4. Select **Enable camera** to open the camera on your computer or mobile device.
5. Scan the QR code on your Keycard Shell with the camera on your computer or mobile device.

<Admonition type="note">
   During verification, the Shell signs a one-time QR challenge and includes its device certificate. The verification service validates the signature and certificate, then returns the verification result and history. You then scan a final QR code with the Shell to confirm that the result is not altered by the browser. Check out [Keycard Shell verification: How you can proof your Shell is authentic ↗](https://keycard.tech/en/blog/keycard-shell-verification-what-verify-actually-proves-and-why-it-matters) to learn more.
</Admonition>

<Admonition type="caution">
    If your Keycard Shell is not authentic, stop using it and contact the Keycard team [on Discord ↗](https://discord.gg/uJAXk7jFhZ).
</Admonition>
