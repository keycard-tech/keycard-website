# Update Keycard Shell in air gapped mode 

Updating your Keycard Shell in air gapped mode helps maintain the security of your device by minimizing its exposure to online threats during the update.

<Admonition type="note">
    To update your Keycard Shell via the browser, read [Update Keycard Shell online](update-keycard-shell-online).
</Admonition>

## Update Keycard Shell in air gapped mode

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer with internet access to download the update files, a USB drive to transfer the files, and an air-gapped computer (a computer that is not connected to the internet).

Make sure you [enable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell before starting the update process.

<Admonition type="note">
   If you use Linux, you must add the following udev rule to the file `/etc/udev/rules.d/50-keycardshell.rules` to grant the required permissions.

   ```Markdown
   ACTION!="remove", KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="21f7", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
   ```
</Admonition>

## Step 1: Download the updater and update files

On your computer with internet access, download the files you need. Your Keycard Shell may already be up to date on the firmware, the database or both.

1. Open the air-gapped update page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped). Before you continue, confirm the URL is exactly `https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped`.
1. Under **Download stand-alone Shell updater app**, choose your operating system in **Select OS**, then click **Download Shell Updater**.
1. Under **Download firmware package**, choose your firmware in **Select variant**, then download it:
    - **omni**: the multi-chain firmware.
    - **btc**: the Bitcoin-only firmware.
1. Under **Download database package**, download the database.
1. Check each downloaded file with `shasum -a 256 <file>` on macOS or Linux, or `certutil -hashfile <file> SHA256` on Windows. For the firmware and the database, the result must be identical to the SHA256 shown on the update page. For the updater, it must be identical to the **digest** shown next to the file on the [Keycard Shell Updater releases page ↗](https://github.com/keycard-tech/kshell-updater/releases/latest).
1. Transfer the downloaded files to your air-gapped computer using a freshly formatted USB drive.

<Admonition type="note">
    You can also download the same files from GitHub: the [updater ↗](https://github.com/keycard-tech/kshell-updater/releases/latest), the [firmware ↗](https://github.com/keycard-tech/keycard-shell/releases/latest) (`shellos-<date>-<version>.bin` for multi-chain, or the file that ends in `btc-only.bin`) and the [database ↗](https://github.com/keycard-tech/keycard-eth-db/releases/latest) (`shelldb-<date>.bin`). GitHub shows the SHA256 of each file next to it as **digest**. It is the same value as on the update page.
</Admonition>

## Step 2: Update your Keycard Shell

<Admonition type="caution">
    Always install the firmware before the database. Databases from 2026-11-11 on are signed with a new key, and only firmware v1.5.3 or later accepts it. If you select both, the updater installs the firmware first.
</Admonition>

1. On your air-gapped computer, install and open Keycard Shell Updater.
1. Connect your Keycard Shell to the computer with a USB-C cable and unlock it. The updater shows **Keycard Shell connected** and the firmware and database versions on your Keycard Shell.
1. To update the firmware, in the **Firmware** section, select the checkbox, click **From file**, then click **Choose a .bin file** and select the firmware file on your USB drive.
1. To update the database, in the **Database** section, select the checkbox, click **From file**, then click **Choose a .bin file** and select the database file on your USB drive.
1. Click **Transfer to device**.
1. On your Keycard Shell, press **OK** to receive the firmware. When the transfer ends, press **OK** again to install it. Your Keycard Shell restarts.
1. Enter your Keycard PIN on your Keycard Shell.
1. If you also selected the database, the updater sends it next. On your Keycard Shell, press **OK** to receive the database. When the transfer ends, press **OK** again to install it.

When the update is complete, the updater shows the firmware and database versions that your Keycard Shell now runs.

<Admonition type="note">
    Do not disconnect your Keycard Shell during the transfer. If you click **Cancel**, the updater stops and writes nothing more to your Keycard Shell. Click **Try again** to restart the transfer.
</Admonition>

<Admonition type="tip">
    After the update, [disable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell to ensure it remains air-gapped.
</Admonition>
