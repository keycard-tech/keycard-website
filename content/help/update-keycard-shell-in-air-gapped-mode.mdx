# Update Keycard Shell in air gapped mode 

<Admonition type="caution">
    Air-gapped updates are paused until the new version of the standalone updater is released. Until then, read [Update Keycard Shell online](update-keycard-shell-online).
</Admonition>

Updating your Keycard Shell in air gapped mode helps maintain the security of your device by minimizing its exposure to online threats during the update.

<Admonition type="note">
    To update your Keycard Shell via the browser, read [Update Keycard Shell online](update-keycard-shell-online).
</Admonition>

## Update Keycard Shell in air gapped mode

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer to access the [update files ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update), a USB drive to transfer the files, and an air-gapped computer (a computer that is not connected to the internet).

Make sure you [enable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell before starting the update process.

<Admonition type="note">
   If you use Linux, you must add the following udev rule to the file `/etc/udev/rules.d/50-keycardshell.rules` to grant the required permissions.

   ```Markdown
   ACTION!="remove", KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="21f7", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
   ```
</Admonition>

## Step 1: Download the updater and update files

The update page displays the latest available firmware and database versions. Your Keycard Shell may already be up to date on one or both of these versions. Download the update you need to install.

1. Open the air-gapped update page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped). Before you continue, confirm the URL is exactly `https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped`.
1. Under **Download firmware package**, choose your variant in **Select variant**, then download the firmware.
1. Under **Download database package**, download the database.
1. Optionally, verify the integrity of the downloaded files using the SHA256 checksums shown on the update page.
1. Transfer the downloaded files to your air-gapped computer using a verified and secure USB drive.

## Step 2: Update your Keycard Shell

<Admonition type="caution">
    Always install the firmware before the database. Databases from 2026-11-11 on are signed with a new key, and only firmware v1.5.3 or later accepts it.
</Admonition>

1. On your air-gapped computer, unzip and run the updater application.
2. Under **Firmware Update**, click **Select local file** and select the firmware file on your USB drive.
3. On your Keycard Shell, press **OK** to receive the firmware. When the transfer ends, press **OK** again to install it. Your Keycard Shell restarts.
4. Enter your Keycard PIN on your Keycard Shell.
5. In the updater, click **Back to home**.
6. Under **Database Update**, click **Select local file** and select the database file on your USB drive.
7. On your Keycard Shell, press **OK** to receive the database. When the transfer ends, press **OK** again to install it.

<Admonition type="tip">
    After the update, [disable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell to ensure it remains air-gapped.
</Admonition>
