# Update Keycard Shell in air gapped mode 

Keycard Shell works without any connection: it talks to your software wallet with QR codes only. A firmware or database update is the exception. It needs a USB connection to a computer.

The [online update](./update-keycard-shell-online) uses a web browser, so the computer must be connected to the internet. To keep that computer offline as well, use **Keycard Shell Updater**, a standalone app that runs on a computer that is not connected to the internet. You download the files on another computer, check them, and bring them over on a USB drive.

## What you need

- Your Keycard and Keycard Shell, and a USB-C cable.
- A computer with internet access, to download the files.
- An air-gapped computer: a computer that is not connected to the internet.
- A freshly formatted USB drive.

## Update your Keycard Shell

1. On your Keycard Shell, [enable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell).
2. Follow the instructions on [https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update/air-gapped).
3. After the update, [disable data transfer](./enable-or-disable-data-transfer-on-keycard-shell) on your Keycard Shell to keep it air-gapped.

<Admonition type="note">
   If your air-gapped computer runs Linux, add this udev rule to the file `/etc/udev/rules.d/50-keycardshell.rules` before you connect your Keycard Shell. It gives the updater access to your Keycard Shell over USB.

   ```Markdown
   ACTION!="remove", KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="21f7", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
   ```
</Admonition>
