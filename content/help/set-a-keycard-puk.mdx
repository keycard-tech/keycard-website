# Set up your PUK

Your Keycard blocks after three wrong PIN attempts.

Unblock with either:
- **[Factory reset](./factory-reset-your-keycard-using-keycard-shell)**: Removes your keys. Import them again with your recovery phrase.
- **PUK**: 12-digit code that unblocks your Keycard without removing keys.

Your card has a random PUK by default.

Change your PUK:
1. Insert Keycard and enter your PIN (card must be unblocked).
2. Go to **Settings** > **Keycard** > **Change PUK**.
