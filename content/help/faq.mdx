# Keycard and Keycard Shell FAQ

## General questions

- **What is the difference between Keycard and Keycard Shell?**

Keycard is a hardware wallet that securely stores the private keys of your crypto wallet. Keycard Shell is a companion device for interacting with wallet apps and signing transactions through QR codes. Check out [About Keycard and Keycard Shell](./about-keycard-and-keycard-shell.mdx) to learn more.

- **Can I use a Keycard or Keycard Shell standalone?**

You can use Keycard without Keycard Shell, if you [use your Keycard along with the Status app](https://status.app/help/keycard/create-a-status-wallet-account-using-a-keycard) ↗. Keycard connects to the Status mobile app via NFC and to the Status desktop app via a USB smart card reader.

You can't use Keycard Shell without Keycard.

- **How many Keycards can I use with one Keycard Shell device?**

Unlimited. Keycard Shell works with any Keycard, including yours and someone else's.

- **Which cryptocurrencies do Keycard and Keycard Shell support?**

Keycard and Keycard Shell support Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and ERC-20 tokens.

- **How many wallets can I use on one Keycard?**

There are unlimited Ethereum and Bitcoin addresses on one Keycard. They are not separate wallets. They are derived from one key pair.

- **Can I use my Keycard or Keycard Shell without a wallet app?**

No, you can't use your Keycard or Keycard Shell without a [compatible wallet app](developers/supported-wallets). Wallet apps provide the interface for managing your crypto.

If you use Keycard alone, you must use the [Status app](https://status.app/help/keycard/create-a-status-wallet-account-using-a-keycard). If you use Keycard and Keycard Shell together, there are [a list of options](developers/supported-wallets) such as MetaMask, Rabby and BlueWallet.

- **Can I use more than one wallet app with the same Keycard?**

Yes, you can connect one Keycard to multiple wallet apps following [the same process](./connect-keycard-shell-to-a-wallet-app.mdx), and use any of them as needed.

- **Can I connect Keycard Shell directly to a dApp?**

Currently, Keycard Shell can't connect directly to dApps. You need to use a [compatible wallet app](/developers/supported-wallets) that supports Keycard and Keycard Shell to interact with dApps.

## Security and support

- **Are Keycard and Keycard Shell air-gapped?**

Keycard and Keycard Shell are not permanently air-gapped by design. Keycard supports NFC communication and smart card readers. Keycard Shell connects to your devices via USB.

You can configure both devices to work together in an air-gapped manner. Check out [Keep your keycard and Keycard Shell air-gapped](./keep-your-keycard-and-keycard-shell-air-gapped.mdx) to learn how.

- **What if I lose my Keycard or Keycard Shell?**

Lost Keycard: Your funds are safe if your Keycard PIN remains private. Use your recovery phrase to restore your wallet on a new device. For maximum security, transfer your funds to a new wallet address immediately after restoration.

Lost Keycard Shell: Your funds are secure because Keycard Shell stores no private keys or sensitive information. You can [purchase a replacement Keycard Shell](https://get.keycard.tech/pages/keycard-shell) to use with your existing Keycard.

- **Can someone copy my Keycard?**

No. Your private keys are on Keycard's secure element chip, which prevents cloning or extraction of the keys by design.

- **What should I do if I forget my Keycard PIN?**

This doesn't affect your access to your funds. You can always access your funds with your recovery phrase.

To reset your Keycard PIN, [factory reset](./factory-reset-your-keycard) your Keycard and then [import your wallet accounts](./set-up-your-keycard.mdx#add-a-key-pair) again. 

- **How do I get support if I have a problem with my Keycard or Keycard Shell?**

If you need help with your Keycard or Keycard Shell, visit the [Help page](https://keycard.tech/help) for guidance. 

You can also ask questions in the [Keycard community](https://discord.gg/uJAXk7jFhZ) on Discord.
