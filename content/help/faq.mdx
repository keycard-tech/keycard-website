# Keycard and Keycard Shell FAQ

## General questions 

- **What is the difference between Keycard and Keycard Shell?**

Keycard stores your private keys. Shell is a companion device that lets you interact with software wallets and sign transactions via QR codes. Learn more: [About Keycard and Shell](./about-keycard-and-keycard-shell).

- **Can Keycard and Shell work independently?**

Yes, you can use Keycard without Shell via [Status app ↗](https://status.app/help/keycard/create-a-status-wallet-account-using-a-keycard) or other [compatible wallets](/wallets). Keycard connects to mobile wallets via NFC and desktop wallets via USB smart card reader.

No, Shell requires Keycard to function.

- **How many Keycards can I use with one Shell?**

Unlimited. You can share one Shell with friends and family because it doesn't store private keys or sensitive information.

- **Which blockchains do Keycard and Shell support?**

Ethereum, all EVM-based blockchains, and Bitcoin. We're actively working on supporting more blockchains. 

- **Is there a bitcoin-only mode?**

No separate firmware. Use Shell with Bitcoin-only wallets (PSBT over QR/UR or USB) and avoid Ethereum features. Advanced users can compile custom firmware that omits ETH data. See [Shell internals](/developers/keycard-shell).

- **How many wallets can I use on one Keycard?**

Your Keycard stores one master key from a [BIP39 or SLIP39](./understand-the-slip-39-wallet-backup-standard) recovery phrase. This generates unlimited Ethereum and Bitcoin addresses ([BIP32 standard ↗](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0032.mediawiki)).

- **Can I use Keycard or Shell without a software wallet?**

No. Software wallets provide the interface for managing crypto, creating transactions, and connecting to the blockchain. See [compatible wallets](https://keycard.tech/en/wallets) (10+ options).

Use Keycard alone with [Status app ↗](https://status.app/help/keycard/create-a-status-wallet-account-using-a-keycard) or [Sparrow](https://sparrowwallet.com/). Use Keycard with Shell for MetaMask, Rabby, Sparrow, and [more](https://keycard.tech/en/wallets).

- **Can I use multiple software wallets with one Shell?**

Yes. [Connect your Shell](./connect-keycard-shell-to-a-software-wallet) to multiple wallets and switch between them.

- **Can I connect Shell directly to a dApp?**

Yes, if the dApp supports QR codes directly (EIP-4527 for EVM or UR for BTC). Always verify what you're signing on your Shell screen—you can't trust what your browser shows. 

- **Can I build my own Shell?**

Yes. Shell is fully open source. DIY builds require your own hardware and firmware signing keys (advanced). See [Build a DIY Shell](/developers/diy-keycard-shell).

## Security and support

- **Are Keycard and Shell air-gapped?**

Shell operates air-gapped via QR codes. To update firmware or database, connect via USB or [update offline](./update-your-keycard-shell-offline). Keycard supports NFC and smart card readers, but these aren't needed with Shell.

- **What if I lose my Keycard or Shell?**

Lost Keycard: Your funds are safe if your PIN stays private. The card blocks after three wrong PIN attempts. Restore your wallet with your recovery phrase on a new device. For maximum security, transfer funds to a new wallet immediately.

Lost Shell: Your funds are secure because Shell stores no private keys or information. [Buy a replacement Shell](https://get.keycard.tech/pages/keycard-shell) to use with your existing Keycard.

- **Can someone copy my Keycard?**

No. The secure element chip prevents key cloning or extraction.

- **Can I run my own firmware on Shell?**

Official Shell devices only accept signed firmware. For custom firmware, build your own device with a bootloader key you control. See [Shell internals](/developers/keycard-shell).

- **What if I forget my PIN?**

Your funds are safe. Access them with your recovery phrase.

To reset your PIN, [factory reset](./factory-reset-your-keycard-using-keycard-shell) your Keycard, then [import your wallet](./set-up-your-keycard-with-keycard-shell#add-a-key-pair) again.
