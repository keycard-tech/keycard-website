# Connect Keycard Shell to Sparrow Wallet

![A header image showing the Sparrow Wallet logo and a Keycard Shell device](/assets/docs/connect-keycard-shell-to-sparrow-wallet-header.png)

You can use your Keycard Shell with Sparrow Wallet to manage crypto assets, interact with dApps, and access all available Sparrow Wallet features.

<Admonition type="note">
    Currently, Keycard Shell doesn't appear on Sparrow Wallet's list of supported hardware wallets, but you can still connect it to Sparrow using the **Keystone** option.
</Admonition>

## What to expect

- You can use Sparrow to [import your regular (single-signature) Bitcoin wallet](#import-keycard-bitcoin-wallet-to-sparrow) or [create a multi-signature wallet](#create-a-multisig-wallet-with-keycard-in-sparrow) that include your Keycard as one of the cosigners.
- If you have multiple Keycards, you can use each of them as separate cosigners in a multisig wallet.
- After connecting to Sparrow, your wallet private keys remain on your Keycard, and Sparrow has no access to them.
- Sparrow is an interface to manage your crypto assets. For any transactions generated during use, sign with Keycard Shell.
- You can still connect your Keycard Shell to other [supported wallet apps](https://keycard.tech/wallets).

## Import Keycard Bitcoin wallet to Sparrow

### Step 1: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on. 
1. Enter your Keycard PIN and press **OK**.
1. Select **Connect software wallet**.
1. Press **OK** to confirm the text on the screen.
1. Select **Bitcoin**.

### Step 2: Connect to Sparrow

1. In Sparrow Wallet on the top bar menu, select **File** > **Import Wallet**.
1. Find **Keystone** and select **Scan** on the right.
1. Scan the QR code displayed on the Keycard Shell screen using your computer camera.
1. In Sparrow Wallet, select script type (Legacy, Nested SegWit, or Native SegWit) and then select **Import**.
1. Enter a name for your wallet and select **Create Wallet**.
1. Optionally, set a password for your wallet and select **Set Password**. To continue without a password, select **No password**.

## Create a multisig wallet with Keycard in Sparrow

### Step 1: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on. 
1. Enter your Keycard PIN and press **OK**.
1. Select **Connect software wallet**.
1. Press **OK** to confirm the text on the screen.
1. Select **Bitcoin MultiSig**.

### Step 2: Create a multisig wallet in Sparrow

1. In Sparrow Wallet on the top bar menu, select **File** > **New Wallet**.
1. Enter a name for your wallet and select **Create Wallet**.
1. For **Policy Type**, select **Multi Signature**. For **Script Type**, select **Legacy**, **Nested SegWit**, or **Native SegWit**.
1. For **Cosigners**, slide to set the number of required signatures and total cosigners.
1. Under **Keystore**, for each cosigner wallet, select **Airgapped Hardware Wallet**. Then find **Keystone multisig** and then select **Scan** on the right.
1. Scan the QR code displayed on the Keycard Shell screen using your computer camera.
1. Optionally, set a password for your wallet and select **Set Password**. To continue without a password, select **No password**.
