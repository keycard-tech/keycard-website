# Update your Keycard Shell online

Regularly updating your Keycard Shell firmware and database keeps your device current with the latest features and security improvements.

<Admonition type="note">
    To update your Keycard Shell in the air-gapped mode, read [Update your Keycard Shell offline](update-your-keycard-shell-offline.mdx).
</Admonition>

## Update your Keycard Shell online

In addition to your Keycard and Keycard Shell, you need a computer to access the [update page ↗](https://shell.keycard.tech/update) to complete the update process.

<Admonition type="note">
   If you use Linux, you must add the following udev rule to the file `/etc/udev/rules.d/50-keycardshell.rules` to grant the required permissions.

   ```Markdown
   ACTION!="remove", KERNEL=="hidraw*", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="21f7", MODE="0660", TAG+="uaccess"
   ```  
</Admonition>

### Step 1: Prepare your Keycard Shell

1. Insert your Keycard into Keycard Shell to power it on.
1. Enter your Keycard PIN.
1. Connect your Keycard Shell to your computer using a USB-C cable.
1. Allow the connection request if you see a security alert from your computer.

### Step 2: Prepare the update page

1. On your computer, open the update page at [https://shell.keycard.tech/update](https://shell.keycard.tech/update).
1. Click **Update**.
1. In the **shell.keycard.tech wants to connect to a HID device** prompt window, select your Keycard Shell device and click **Connect**.
1. The latest firmware and database version are automatically selected. Unselect if you don't want to install one of the updates.

### Step 3: Update your Keycard Shell

You need to confirm each update individually.

1. On the update web page, click **Transfer to device** to transfer the database update to your Keycard Shell.
1. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the database update by pressing **OK**.
1. Return to the update web page, click **Transfer to device** again to transfer the firmware update to your Keycard Shell.
1. On your Keycard Shell, confirm the firmware update by pressing **OK**.
1. Press **OK** again.
