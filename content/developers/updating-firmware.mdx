# Updating Firmware

To update Keycard’s firmware, use the [Keycard CLI](https://github.com/keycard-tech/keycard-cli) with a USB reader. Download the latest applet version from [GitHub](https://github.com/keycard-tech/status-keycard/releases) and follow the reinstallation scripts.

To update Keycard Shell’s firmware, we will provide a service soon.
