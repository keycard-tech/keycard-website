# Hardware Specification

Complete hardware characteristics of both devices. Keycard and Keycard Shell are open hardware: schematics, bill of materials and 3D files are published in the [keycard-shell repository](https://github.com/keycard-tech/keycard-shell), and the official certification documents are [available on GitHub](https://github.com/keycard-tech/keycard-shell/tree/master/hardware/shell/serial_run/CERTIFICATION-OFFICIALCERTIFICATES).

## Keycard Shell

<Table>
    <TableHead>
        <TableHeader>Characteristic</TableHeader>
        <TableHeader>Specification</TableHeader>
    </TableHead>

    <TableContent>
        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Microcontroller</TableCell>
            <TableCell>STM32H573, PSA Level 3 and SESIP3 certified ([PSA certificate](https://www.psacertified.org/products/stm32h57x/))</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Display</TableCell>
            <TableCell>2.0" TFT IPS colour screen, 240 × 320 pixels, adjustable brightness</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Input</TableCell>
            <TableCell>12-key keypad ('0'–'9', Cancel, OK — hard rubber with durable coating) + on/off button</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Camera</TableCell>
            <TableCell>CMOS with global shutter — captures the full frame at once, required for reliable animated QR scanning</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Card reader</TableCell>
            <TableCell>ISO 7816 smartcard reader (contact only, no NFC)</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>USB</TableCell>
            <TableCell>USB-C; works with any charger (PD or non-PD); data lines can be disabled in software</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Radio</TableCell>
            <TableCell>None — no WiFi, Bluetooth or cellular. Air-gapped operation: no RF hardware + USB data off switch</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Battery</TableCell>
            <TableCell>Nokia BL-4C 800 mAh Li-Po — user-replaceable without tools, ~$3 worldwide, form factor available for 25+ years</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Flash memory</TableCell>
            <TableCell>2 MB dual-bank: 32 KB bootloader + 608 KB firmware + 384 KB data per bank</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Boot</TableCell>
            <TableCell>20 ms boot time; auto on/off at card insertion</TableCell>
        </TableRow>

        <TableRow>
            <TableCell>Enclosure</TableCell>
            <TableCell>ABS plastic, dust resistant, pocket-sized, no branding or crypto identifiers</TableCell>
        </TableRow>
    </TableContent>
</Table>

For firmware architecture, USB protocol and flash layout details, see [Keycard Shell internals](/developers/keycard-shell).

<Admonition type="note">
  PSA Level 3 imposes 35 days of white-box testing, vulnerability analysis and
  penetration testing to verify that a chip's PSA Root of Trust can withstand
  substantial hardware and software attacks, including physical tampering and
  side-channel attacks.
</Admonition>
