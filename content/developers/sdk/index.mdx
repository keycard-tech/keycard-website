<Admonition type="note">
  This documentation uses the **Java SDK** as the reference: it is maintained
  by the Keycard team, covers the full protocol, and runs on desktop (via
  `javax.smartcardio` and a USB reader) and Android (via NFC). The concepts in
  these pages — connection, pairing, secure channel, derivation, signing — map
  directly onto every other SDK. If your language has no SDK yet, the [Keycard
  API](/developers/apdu) documents the underlying protocol.
</Admonition>
