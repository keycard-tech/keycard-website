# Hardware Specification

## Keycard

Keycard applet runs on any JavaCard 3.0.4+ (see [applet installation](https://keycard.tech/docs/applet_installation))

We use for manufacturing:
- **model:** NXP JCOP4
- **interfaces:** NFC, ISO7816
- **EAL5+ certified**

## Keycard Shell

- **interfaces:** 12 keys keyboard, on/off button, TFT color display, 900mAh BL-4C battery, USBC port, smartcard reader
- **more details:** on our github[repo](https://github.com/keycard-tech/keycard-pro/).
